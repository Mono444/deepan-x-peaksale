Privacy Policy

PeakSales Private Limited ("PeakSales", "we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who visits this website or submits an enquiry or job application.

What we collect

When you book a discovery call or submit a job application, we collect the information you provide: name, email address, phone number, and any other details you choose to share.

We may also collect standard analytics data (pages visited, time on page, device type) through tools such as Google Analytics 4.

How we use your data

  • To respond to your enquiry or process your application
  • To schedule and conduct discovery calls
  • To send relevant follow-up communications (you can opt out at any time)
  • To improve the website using aggregated, anonymised analytics

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers necessary to run our business (for example, Cal.com for appointment scheduling, and a form provider for job applications). Those providers handle data under their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act you have the right to access, correct and erase personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address in our company facts block once it is confirmed.

Cookies

This site may use cookies for analytics. A cookie banner will be added before any advertising pixels are activated.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact PeakSales Private Limited, Trichy, Tamil Nadu. Email address to be confirmed.
