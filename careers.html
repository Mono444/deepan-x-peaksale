Questions candidates ask

Do I need sales experience?

No. Caller roles are open to freshers. We provide training, scripts and daily coaching from your team lead.

What will I sell?

Products and services from the businesses we work with. You will be assigned to one or more brands and trained on each.

How does training work?

You get product training, call scripts and role-play sessions before your first live call. Your team lead reviews your calls and helps you improve every week.

Will I get real leads?

Yes. You work on inbound leads from real businesses, not cold lists or bought databases.

How long is the hiring process?

You apply (3 minutes), we call you for a short screening, and if it is a fit, you get an offer. The whole process is usually quick.

Can I grow into a closer or team lead role?

Yes. Callers who perform well move to closer roles, and closers can grow into team leads who manage their own pod.

Not sure you are ready?

Read the candidate FAQ above or just apply. No experience is required for caller roles and we train everyone from day one.
