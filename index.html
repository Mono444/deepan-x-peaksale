Frequently asked questions.

How does pricing work?
It depends on the model. Dedicated Sales Team: a base retainer plus commission on closed and collected revenue. Full-Service Growth: a monthly retainer plus a performance share. Sales Optimisation: a fixed fee or retainer. We agree the numbers on the discovery call.
What industries do you work with?
B2B businesses such as SaaS, EdTech, professional services and agencies. If your leads convert through calls and follow-ups, we can help.
How quickly can you start?
Within 5–7 business days after scope and onboarding are agreed.
How is this different from hiring our own team?
An in-house team means months of hiring, training and management. We bring a trained team and our own management from day one.
Do I need to provide leads?
You can bring your own: enquiries, marketing leads or an existing database. We convert them. Lead generation is part of Full-Service Growth and can be discussed separately.
How do you report?
Weekly: call volume, follow-ups, lead status, conversion rate and pipeline health.
